The Origin
"The retreat in Bali was
well-intentioned.
But the medicine was wrong.
The standards were wrong.
We knew we could
do far better."

Drew Fleming, Co-Founder

In 2023, Drew Fleming took a group of clients on a wellness retreat to Bali. The setting was beautiful. The intentions were genuine. But when it came to the actual practice — the herbal formulas, the diagnostic methods, the depth of the healing tradition — it fell short. The products were generic. The medicine wasn't Tibetan. It was theatre.

He came home with a conviction: that Tibetan medicine — one of the world's most complete and sophisticated healing traditions — deserved an institution worthy of it. One grounded in authentic lineage, led by practitioners of genuine expertise, and held to a standard that both the ancient tradition and modern science could respect.

Three Roots was founded on that conviction. Not a retreat company. Not a wellness brand. A movement to bring one of humanity's greatest healing traditions to the people who need it most.

Our Research

Ancient knowledge.
Modern evidence.
Uncompromised standards.

Tibetan medicine does not need to be proven — it has been practised and refined for 4,000 years. But for it to reach the people who need it in the West, it must speak the language of evidence. That is what our research programme does.

Areas of Study

What we are
documenting.

Area 01

Medicinal Formulas

Clinical efficacy studies of classical Tibetan herbal formulas — tracking patient outcomes, biomarker changes, and quality-of-life metrics across our retreat populations.

Area 02

Diagnostic Methods

Correlation studies between Tibetan pulse and urine diagnosis and Western biomedical findings — building the bridge between classical and contemporary diagnostic paradigms.

Area 03

Tibetan Yoga Outcomes

Physiological and psychological outcome measurement for Yantra Yoga practice — stress markers, neurological correlates, and longitudinal wellbeing data.

Area 04

External Therapies

Documenting the clinical outcomes of Tibetan massage, medicinal baths, moxibustion, golden needle therapy, and other classical external treatment modalities.

Area 05

Mental Health Applications

Tibetan medicine has sophisticated frameworks for understanding psychological suffering. We are studying outcomes in anxiety, depression, and burnout across our patient cohort.

Area 06

Integrative Protocols

Developing documented protocols for integrating Tibetan medicine alongside conventional Western treatment — for patients managing chronic conditions with medical oversight.

Our Standard

Not alternative.
Complementary.
Fully accredited.

Our long-term goal is the accreditation of our in-house manufacturing processes to Western GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) standards — enabling the international distribution of authentic Tibetan medicinal products for the first time.

This is not a small ambition. It has never been done. It requires a sustained programme of research, regulatory engagement, and institutional relationship-building. Three Roots is built to do exactly that.

In-House Clinical Documentation

Full patient records, treatment protocols, and outcome tracking from day one of operation. Building the evidence base systematically, from the ground up.

Western Scientific Partnerships

Collaborations with university research departments and independent clinical scientists to provide rigorous external validation of our in-house findings.

GMP Manufacturing Roadmap

A phased programme toward Good Manufacturing Practice certification for our medicinal formulas — the key to Western market access for Tibetan medicines.

Publication & Dissemination

Peer-reviewed publications, white papers, and public-facing documentation of our research outcomes — contributing to the global evidence base for Tibetan medicine.

The Philosophy

Not supplements.
Classical formulas.
Properly made.

Tibetan medicine has a pharmacopoeia of thousands of formulas, refined over centuries. They are not herbal supplements. They are precise, multi-ingredient preparations with specific indications, dosages, and preparation methods — prescribed following diagnosis, not marketed broadly.

What Three Roots is building is the pathway to bring these formulas — properly authenticated, properly manufactured, properly studied — to practitioners and patients in the West who have no current access to them. It is a significant undertaking. That is exactly why we are doing it.
