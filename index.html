The Origin
"I spent a decade
studying this medicine —
in the Himalayas

what this tradition knows."

Drew McGlathery, Co-Founder & CEO

Three Roots began with a scholar's journey. As a Nehru-Fulbright Senior Scholar, Drew McGlathery spent a decade studying medicine across the Himalayas and the United States — training with some of the finest doctors in both worlds, from leading Western clinicians to the masters of Tibetan medicine at Mentseekhang, the medical institute of His Holiness the Dalai Lama.

What he found there went far beyond technique. Rooted in Tibetan Buddhism and centuries of meditative practice, this medicine understands the human being as a whole — body, mind, and spirit — with a depth and completeness that changed the course of his life.

Three Roots was founded to carry that wisdom forward. Its co-founders share one passion and one commitment: to bring one of humanity's greatest healing traditions to the world — in service of people and the planet.

The Vision

Where medicine
and mountain meet.

Dharamshala was chosen with care. It is the home of His Holiness the Dalai Lama and of one of the world's most vibrant living communities of Tibetan medical knowledge. Building here, in the heart of the tradition, keeps Three Roots close to the teachers, the lineage, and the living practice that give this medicine its depth.

Clients will come to Three Roots for a defined residential programme — typically 7 to 21 days — receiving full Tibetan medical consultations, personalised treatment plans, medicinal therapies, yoga instruction, and deep meditation practice.

Every element will be grounded in authentic clinical practice, overseen by practitioners of genuine lineage, and documented for our ongoing research programme.

Our Research

Ancient knowledge.
Modern evidence.
Uncompromised standards.

Tibetan medicine has been practised and refined for more than 2,000 years. For it to reach the people who need it in the West, it must also speak the language of evidence — and that is the work of our research programme.

Areas of Study

What we are
documenting.

Area 01

Medicinal Formulas

Clinical efficacy studies of classical Tibetan herbal formulas — tracking patient outcomes, biomarker changes, and quality-of-life metrics across our retreat populations.

Area 02

Diagnostic Methods

Correlation studies between Tibetan pulse and urine diagnosis and Western biomedical findings — building the bridge between classical and contemporary diagnostic paradigms.

Area 03

Tibetan Yoga Outcomes

Physiological and psychological outcome measurement for Yantra Yoga practice — stress markers, neurological correlates, and longitudinal wellbeing data.

Area 04

External Therapies

Documenting the clinical outcomes of Tibetan massage, medicinal baths, moxibustion, golden needle therapy, and other classical external treatment modalities.

Area 05

Mental Health Applications

Tibetan medicine has sophisticated frameworks for understanding psychological suffering. We are studying outcomes in anxiety, depression, and burnout across our patient cohort.

Area 06

Integrative Protocols

Developing documented protocols for integrating Tibetan medicine alongside conventional Western treatment — for patients managing chronic conditions with medical oversight.

Our long-term goal is the accreditation of our in-house manufacturing processes to Western GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) standards — enabling the international distribution of authentic Tibetan medicinal products for the first time.

It is an ambition worthy of the tradition itself — a sustained programme of research, regulatory engagement, and institutional relationship-building. Three Roots was built for exactly this work.

The Philosophy

Not supplements.
Classical formulas.
Properly made.

Tibetan medicine has a pharmacopoeia of thousands of formulas, refined over centuries. They are not herbal supplements. They are precise, multi-ingredient preparations with specific indications, dosages, and preparation methods — prescribed following diagnosis, not marketed broadly.

What Three Roots is building is the pathway to bring these formulas — properly authenticated, properly manufactured, properly studied — to practitioners and patients in the West who have no current access to them. It is a significant undertaking. That is exactly why we are doing it.
