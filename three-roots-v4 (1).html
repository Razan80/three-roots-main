The Origin
"The retreat in Bali was
well-intentioned.
But the medicine was wrong.
The standards were wrong.

Drew McGlathery, Founder & CEO

In 2023, Drew McGlathery took a group of clients on a wellness retreat to Bali. The setting was beautiful. The intentions were genuine. But when it came to the actual practice — the herbal formulas, the diagnostic methods, the depth of the healing tradition — it fell short. The products were generic. The medicine wasn't Tibetan. It was theatre.

He came home with a conviction: that Tibetan medicine — one of the world's most complete and sophisticated healing traditions — deserved an institution worthy of it. One grounded in authentic lineage, led by practitioners of genuine expertise, and held to a standard that both the ancient tradition and modern science could respect.

Three Roots was founded on that conviction. Not a retreat company. Not a wellness brand. A movement to bring one of humanity's greatest healing traditions to the people who need it most.

When these elements are in harmony within the body, vitality flourishes. When imbalanced, illness arises.

This medical system is not just about treating symptoms — it is a deeply holistic approach to restoring balance in the body, mind, and spirit.

An ancient system of movement, breath, and awareness rooted in Tibetan tradition. The mind is likened to a rider on a horse — Trulkor tames the breath and energy channels, creating conditions for mental and physical wellness to arise naturally.

Pulse & Tongue Diagnosis

Classical Tibetan pulse diagnosis directly assesses the health of solid and hollow organs, inflammations, and imbalances. Tongue and urine analysis reveals chronic health concerns. Combined with modern lab biomarkers for a complete picture.

The Philosophy

Not supplements.
Classical formulas.
Properly made.

Tibetan medicine has a pharmacopoeia of thousands of formulas, refined over centuries. They are not herbal supplements. They are precise, multi-ingredient preparations with specific indications, dosages, and preparation methods — prescribed following diagnosis, not marketed broadly.

What Three Roots is building is the pathway to bring these formulas — properly authenticated, properly manufactured, properly studied — to practitioners and patients in the West who have no current access to them. It is a significant undertaking. That is exactly why we are doing it.
